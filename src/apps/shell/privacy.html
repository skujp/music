Privacy Policy
Last updated: 11/23/2026

B3 Bass Boom Box Shell, also known as The B3S, was developed by TheArt using bass.js library created by scriptor. This project is currently maintained at WonderLab123.

1. Information We Collect
The B3S does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
The B3S operates entirely on the user’s device and does not require account creation, login, or identity information.

2. Local Device Data
Any data generated by The B3S (such as processed sheetmusic, settings, or preferences, temporary files) are stored locally on the user’s device.
This data is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

3. Internet Access
The B3S works entirely offline and does not require an internet connection for any of its features.
The B3S does not send or receive data over the internet under any circumstances.

4. Third‑Party Services
The B3S does not integrate with analytics services, advertising networks, tracking tools, or external SDKs that collect personal data.

5. Children’s Privacy
Because The B3S does not collect personal information, it is safe for use by individuals of all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Limitation of Liability
The B3S is intended to support music learning and practice. Results may vary between users.
The developer is not responsible for any issues caused by misuse of The B3S or technical problems.

7. Changes to This Policy
If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this same URL.
Continued use of The B3S after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
